- test: trained_dqn_greedy cuts in 95.3% of episodes, cuts immediately in 27.3%, and reaches terminal liquidation in 12.6%.

- test: the pilot DQN mean reward is below hold_to_terminal in these existing baseline summaries.
- validation: trained_dqn_greedy cuts in 94.4% of episodes, cuts immediately in 27.7%, and reaches terminal liquidation in 13.1%.
- validation: mean DQN episode length is 12.2301 steps versus 102.1463 for hold_to_terminal (ratio 0.1197).
- validation: the pilot DQN mean reward is below hold_to_terminal in these existing baseline summaries.
- This inspection should be read as a mechanical behavior check before deciding whether to train beyond 500 episodes, not as final evidence.
